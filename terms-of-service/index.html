Using this site.

Pages and guides give general information about rodents and their control. They are not medical, legal or engineering advice, and they cannot replace an inspection of your property.

After a walk-through you receive a written price that itemizes sealing, cleanup and repair. Arrival windows depend on the calendar and the travel distance, and the dispatcher states them on the call, with no guarantee beyond what is said.
