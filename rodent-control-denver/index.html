Turning an address into a visit plan.

A city this large holds houses, row homes, apartments, offices and warehouses. We have no picture of your block, so we ask if the building shares a wall, if the unit has a basement and if an alley or drainage line runs behind it.

Some rodent trouble starts next door. If you share a wall, fence or drainage line with a neighbor, say so, because a plan that ignores the shared edge leaves half the route open.

Why timing matters on the Front Range.

Wind and snow can bury a vent cover or foundation gap for weeks and uncover it when the melt comes. Check ground-level openings again after the snow is gone.

Shared walls and bigger buildings.

Inside a multi-unit building a rodent route can run through the voids between apartments, so treating one unit rarely settles things. Renters should name whoever manages the building. With the manager's consent the technician can look at neighboring spaces and the shared utility lines.

Bigger buildings add loading docks, trash rooms and storage cages. Name the dock, the dumpster pad and any basement storage, and the walk-through opens at those spots.

Four steps, one plan.

Expect four stages: a look at the property, traps on the active lanes, metal over each opening and a return trip. Read about trapping and sealing for detail. We return after the first round to check traps and test the repairs.

Cleanup in the right order

Hurrying a cleanup can do more harm than the mess. Keep to the order shown.

  • Skip brooms and vacuums entirely. They throw dry droppings into the air you breathe, so they stay in the closet.
  • Open what you can, walk outside and wait roughly thirty minutes while stale air clears.
  • Wear disposable gloves throughout and wash with soap and running water when they come off.
  • Drench droppings and nests, and the surface under them, with disinfectant or a bleach mix. Let it work for about five minutes.
  • Lift a dead rodent into a bag with a gloved hand or a tool, then seal it together with the used towels before the bin.
  • Fever, aches or shortness of breath within a few weeks of cleaning calls for a doctor. Say what you handled. A pest company cannot advise on health.
