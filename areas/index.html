A town is listed only when two sources agree, the coverage data we hold and a distance check run on its own from Golden. Both have to put the ZIP code within roughly fifteen miles of the city center.

The rule drops a few addresses you might expect. Certain Denver, Broomfield and Thornton ZIP codes fall a hair beyond the line, and each page lists the codes that did qualify.

Cities, towns and in-between.

Idledale, Kittredge, Indian Hills, Evergreen and Eldorado Springs count as census-designated places, a label for communities with no city government of their own. Wheat Ridge, Arvada, Morrison, Littleton, Westminster, Englewood and Commerce City are home rule cities or municipalities. Denver and Broomfield are each a combined city and county.

Not all fourteen lie in Jefferson County. Littleton and Englewood are mainly in Arapahoe County, Commerce City is in Adams County, Eldorado Springs is in Boulder County, and Westminster and Arvada cross county lines. Each page labels its place accordingly.

Range questions.

What does a distance figure mean?

It is the straight line between two ZIP code centers. Roads are longer than that, so the dispatcher agrees an arrival window with you by phone.

Mine is not listed. May I phone anyway?

Yes. Read out the address. A few codes lie just past the range, and the dispatcher will tell you straight whether we can come.

Are ZIP codes 80401 and 80439 handled as Golden?

Not as Golden. 80401 is not listed under Golden in our coverage data, and 80439 appears on the Evergreen page. Call with the street address and we confirm.

Why does Denver show so many ZIP codes?

Denver is far bigger than the part we reach. We list only ZIP codes inside the range and confirm each address by street, since a Denver mailing name can cover neighboring cities.
