Putting services together.

Most jobs mix two or three. The usual chain is inspection, trapping, sealing and a return visit. Cleanup and repair come last, because new insulation is wasted while animals still roam the space.

With a separate price on every stage, trapping and sealing can go ahead this week while cleanup or repair wait a month.

What we leave to other trades.

There is no separate urgent page. Urgent calls ring the same line and get the first opening. Questions about roofing, health and structural repairs with no rodent connection belong with other trades, which we point out when they arise.
